The Finishing Work Your Developer Didn't Do

Most Doncaster new-builds are sold as "complete" but actually have specific gaps that catch homeowners out in the first year. We do all of them — and we know exactly what to look for on each major Doncaster estate, because we've worked across them all.

  • Full bathroom tiling — most new-build bathrooms are only splash-back tiled. We finish the walls properly (£1,500 typical for a standard family-bathroom job)
  • Shower over bath retrofit — bath only? Add a thermostatic shower + glass screen so the whole family has options (£280–£450)
  • Designer radiator swaps — out with the developer's generic radiator, in with a column or vertical designer rad (£250–£600 fitted)
  • Outside taps — developers usually skip these. We fit a proper isolated outside tap (£80–£150)
  • Hard-water filter or softener — Doncaster mains is genuinely hard. Protects the new boiler, dishwasher and shower head from limescale (£400–£900 installed)
  • First annual boiler service — most new-build boiler warranties REQUIRE an annual service from year 1 to stay valid. £95 inc VAT, 40 mins on site, fully Gas Safe registered.
  • Towel rails & heated mirrors — small upgrades that make the new bathroom feel finished (£140–£280)
  • Garden taps, water filters, second basin pluming — anything the developer left as "future-proof" but unfinished
