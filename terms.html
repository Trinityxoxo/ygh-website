6a. Scope of our Boiler Service

Yorkshire Green Heating Ltd’s £90 inc VAT Boiler Service is a combustion-led annual service carried out by a Gas Safe registered engineer in line with manufacturer guidance, BS 7967 (use of portable combustion analysers) and Gas Safe Register Technical Bulletin TB 143 (CO and CO/CO₂ ratio checks). The service is scheduled for 40 minutes on site.

Always included as standard:

  • Visual inspection of the boiler casing, pipework, flue and surrounding area for damage, corrosion or wear.
  • Operational checks — start-up sequence, hot water, heating, controls and thermostat.
  • System pressure check and gas inlet working pressure check.
  • Inspection of all visible seals, joints and connections for gas or water leaks.
  • Flue terminal inspection (internal and external where safely accessible).
  • Combustion / flue gas analysis using a calibrated analyser, including CO level and CO/CO₂ ratio measured against manufacturer tolerance and the TB 143 action levels.
  • Written service report.

Full strip-down — performed only when required. In line with manufacturer service guidance (for example Worcester Bosch’s published service intervals) and TB 143, a full strip-down — removal and cleaning of the burner, heat exchanger and internal components — is only required when combustion readings fall outside the manufacturer’s tolerance, or where visual inspection identifies a fault. Modern condensing boilers typically only require a strip-down every 4–7 years.

Additional time, parts and exclusions. If a strip-down, remedial work or replacement parts are required, we will explain why, quote any additional time and parts before proceeding, and bill any extra time at our standard rate of £114 inc VAT per hour. Parts are quoted separately. The £90 fee does not include: a strip-down where combustion readings are within tolerance and no fault is visible; repairs requiring replacement parts; or manufacturer warranty registration / benchmark schedule completion (please request if required).
