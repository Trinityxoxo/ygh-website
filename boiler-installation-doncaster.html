Types of Boiler We Install in Doncaster

Combi Boilers (Most Popular)

Combi boilers heat water on demand — no hot-water cylinder needed. They're the most popular choice for Doncaster homes because they save space, reduce energy waste, and deliver instant hot water. Ideal for 1–3 bedroom properties with one bathroom. We fit combis from Baxi, Viessmann, Vaillant, Worcester Bosch and Glow-worm, typically within a single day.

System Boilers

For larger Doncaster homes with two or more bathrooms, a system boiler paired with a hot-water cylinder delivers better flow to multiple outlets at once. System boilers work with a sealed circuit — no cold-water tanks in the loft — and are a great mid-range option between combis and traditional boilers.

Heat-Only (Regular) Boilers

Heat-only boilers are common in older Doncaster properties with existing tank-fed systems. If your home already has a hot-water cylinder and a cold-water tank, replacing like-for-like can be the simplest and most cost-effective option. We can also advise on upgrading to a combi or system boiler if that would suit you better.

Signs You Need a New Boiler

Not sure whether to repair or replace? Here are the most common signals that a new boiler installation makes more sense than another repair:

Age: Boilers over 12–15 years old are significantly less efficient than modern condensing models. Replacing an old G-rated boiler with a new A-rated unit can cut your gas bill by up to 30%.
Frequent breakdowns: If you're calling for repairs every winter, the costs soon add up. A new boiler comes with a manufacturer warranty (typically 5–10 years) and far fewer headaches.
Uneven heating: Rooms that won't warm up, radiators with cold patches, or long waits for hot water often point to a boiler that's struggling.
Rising energy bills: An inefficient boiler burns more gas to produce the same heat. A modern replacement pays for itself through lower bills over time.
Strange noises: Kettling, banging or whistling from the boiler usually indicates limescale buildup or internal component failure.
