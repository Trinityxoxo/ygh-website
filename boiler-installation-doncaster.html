Types of Boiler We Install

Combi Boilers

Combi (combination) boilers are the most popular choice for UK homes. They provide both heating and hot water on demand without needing a separate hot water tank. This saves space and energy. Combi boilers are ideal for smaller homes and flats where space is at a premium. They're also very efficient and can significantly reduce your energy bills.

System Boilers

System boilers work with a sealed system and require a separate hot water cylinder. They're excellent for larger homes with multiple bathrooms where you need plenty of hot water simultaneously. System boilers can provide better performance for high-demand situations than combi boilers.

Heat-Only (Regular) Boilers

Traditional heat-only boilers work with open vented tanks. They're common in older homes and can be the best choice if you already have this system installed. They're reliable and can provide excellent heat distribution throughout large properties.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a boiler installation take?
Most boiler installations take 1-2 days. Simple replacements might take just one day, while more complex work with system changes can take longer. We'll give you a clear timeline when we quote.
How much does a new boiler cost?
Boiler costs vary based on the type and brand. A new combi boiler typically costs £1,500-£2,500 installed. We provide free quotes with no obligation.
Do you provide warranties?
Yes. Boilers come with manufacturer warranties (typically 2-10 years). We also stand behind our installation work.
Will a new boiler reduce my energy bills?
Modern boilers are significantly more efficient than older models. A new boiler can save 20-30% on heating costs compared to a 15+ year old boiler.
What paperwork do I get after installation?
You'll receive a Gas Safe installation certificate, manufacturer documentation, and details about your boiler warranty and servicing.
