Common Emergencies We Handle

Burst Pipes

A burst pipe can cause catastrophic water damage in minutes. Our engineers can quickly locate the problem, shut off the water supply, and repair or replace the damaged section. We'll also help you clean up and advise on damage prevention measures.

Leaks and Water Damage

Whether it's a small persistent leak or water pouring through the ceiling, we find the source and fix it. We can also help with moisture control and mold prevention advice.

No Hot Water

Waking up to no hot water is miserable. We diagnose and repair boiler and heating system faults quickly. Most hot water problems can be resolved in a single visit.

Gas Leaks

Never ignore the smell of gas. If you suspect a gas leak, leave the building immediately and call us. We're Gas Safe registered and equipped to handle gas emergencies safely.

Blocked Drains

Backed-up sewage is a health hazard and a nightmare. We clear blockages fast using modern equipment and techniques, leaving your drains flowing freely.

Boiler Breakdowns

A failed boiler in winter is an emergency. We repair boilers on the same day you call, or provide temporary heating solutions if needed.

What To Do In a Plumbing Emergency

Step 1: Stay Calm and Safe — If there's gas involved, evacuate the building. Don't use electrical switches or create sparks.

Step 2: Turn Off the Water — If there's flooding, locate the main water shut-off valve and turn it off. This is usually under the sink, in the basement, or outside by the street.

Step 3: Contain the Damage — Place towels or buckets under leaks to catch water. Move valuable items away from wet areas.

Step 4: Call Us Immediately — Ring 01302 963007 right away. Describe the problem clearly so we can bring the right equipment.

Step 5: Document for Insurance — If there's damage, take photos for your insurance claim. Keep all receipts for repairs.
