Mobility Bathrooms Guide
Bath to shower conversion in Doncaster — what to expect, what it costs, and how to choose a fitter
Real 2026 prices · Free home survey · No pressure, no upselling

If you (or a parent, or someone you care for) is finding it harder to get in and out of the bath, you're not alone. It's one of the most common reasons we get called out in Doncaster — usually after a near-miss, sometimes after a fall. The good news: replacing a bath with a proper walk-in shower is usually a 2-3 day job, costs less than people fear, and changes how independent someone feels in their own home almost immediately.

This is the honest guide. Written by a local Gas Safe engineer who has done dozens of these conversions across Doncaster, Bessacarr, Bawtry, Tickhill and the surrounding DN postcodes. No high-pressure tactics, no "today only" pricing, no jargon. Just what to expect, what it costs in 2026, and the questions worth asking before you commit.

Why People in Doncaster Are Switching

Most of the customers we do this for fall into one of three groups:

  • Older homeowners (typically 65+) who can still manage day-to-day but find stepping over the bath edge has become a real risk — especially first thing in the morning, or with arthritis, knee or hip problems.
  • People recovering from surgery or illness (hip replacement, stroke, fall) who need an accessible bathroom either temporarily or permanently.
  • Family members looking ahead — children helping an elderly parent stay independent at home, often before a problem becomes a crisis.

The decision is rarely about the bath itself. It's about staying safe, staying confident, and staying in your own home. A properly fitted walk-in shower removes the single biggest fall hazard in most houses — the bath edge — and replaces it with a level entry and a proper grab rail.

What "Bath to Shower Conversion" Actually Involves

Here's what we do, step by step, on a typical Doncaster job. Knowing the process up front means no surprises and no salesman talk.

Day 1 — Removal and prep

We arrive in dust sheets and floor protectors. The old bath comes out — usually in one or two pieces — along with the old taps and waste. We isolate the water, check the pipework underneath (older Doncaster terraces sometimes hide a surprise), and make sure the floor structure is sound before anything new goes in. Old tiles around the bath surround come off if they're going to clash with the new shower.

Day 2 — Tray, screen, plumbing

A level-access shower tray gets fitted to the floor — that's the bit that means no step to climb over. We re-route the plumbing to feed the new shower, fit the mixer valve (thermostatic, so the temperature can't suddenly jump), the shower rail with adjustable head, and the toughened glass screen. Grab rails are fitted where you want them, at the height that's right for you.

Day 3 — Tiling, sealing, sign-off

New wall tiles or panels around the shower, silicone sealing, and a proper test of everything before we leave. We walk you through how to use the new thermostat, where the isolation valves are, and what to do if anything ever drips. Final clean — including the dust we made — and that's the job done.

Most jobs run 2-3 days in total. Larger jobs (full bathroom refits, wet rooms, structural changes) can take 4-6 days. Either way, you're without the bathroom for the duration, so most customers arrange to stay with family or use a downstairs cloakroom while we're in.

  • Basic bath-to-shower conversion (remove bath, fit level-access tray and screen, new thermostatic shower, one grab rail, basic tiling around shower) — £2,200 to £2,800 fully fitted
  • Mid-range conversion (above plus full wall panels or proper tiling, two grab rails, fold-down seat, non-slip flooring) — £2,800 to £3,800 fully fitted
  • Premium / wet room conversion (above plus tanking the floor, level wet-room finish, designer fittings, full re-tile) — £3,800 to £5,500 fully fitted

If you also want the basin and toilet replaced at the same time, expect another £900 to £1,400 on top — but it's almost always cheaper to do it all together than to come back six months later.

We give every customer a written, fixed-price quote after a free home survey. The price you sign is the price you pay. If we find something unexpected once we lift the bath out (rotten floorboards, old corroded pipework), we'll pause, show you, explain the options, and only continue once you say so.

The 6 Questions to Ask Any Fitter Before You Commit

Whether you go with us, with another local family-run firm, or with one of the big national mobility companies, ask these six questions. The answers tell you whether you're dealing with someone who'll do a proper job or someone who'll cause problems six months down the line.

1. Are you Gas Safe registered (if any plumbing or hot-water work is involved)?

Bathroom plumbing crosses into pressurised hot-water systems. The fitter doing the work should be Gas Safe registered. Yorkshire Green Heating registration number: 638592 — verifiable on the Gas Safe Register website.

2. Do you give a fixed, written price after the survey?

"From £X" prices on adverts mean nothing. The legitimate fitters all do a free home survey, then send you a written quote with a fixed total. If someone won't put the price in writing before work starts, walk away.

3. What's your guarantee?

Our workmanship guarantee is 5 years on the install plus the manufacturer's warranty on the fittings themselves (typically 10 years on the tray and screen, 5 years on the mixer valve). Some national firms offer "lifetime" guarantees that don't survive a change of ownership — read the small print.

4. Will the same person who quotes me actually do the work?

This matters. We're a small family-run team — Adam quotes most jobs himself and is on site for most installs. Some national firms send a salesperson, then subcontract the actual work to whoever's local that week. Different people on different days is where quality drops.

5. Can I see real photos of jobs you've done in Doncaster?

Not stock photos. Real ones, from real customers, ideally with reviews attached. Our Checkatrade profile has 597 verified Doncaster reviews and hundreds of real job photos: checkatrade.com/trades/yorkshiregreenheating.

6. What's the deposit, and what happens if I change my mind?

Our deposit is typically 25% on materials order, with the balance on completion. Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations, you have a 14-day cooling-off period if you signed the contract at home, and we honour that completely. Anyone asking for 50%+ up front is being too aggressive on cashflow.

Funding Help — What's Available in Doncaster

Most customers pay for these conversions privately, but you might be eligible for help:

  • Disabled Facilities Grant (DFG) — administered by Doncaster Council. Up to £30,000 toward adaptations that help disabled people live independently. There's a means test, but it's worth applying — we can provide written quotes for the application. Apply at: doncaster.gov.uk (search "Disabled Facilities Grant").
  • Doncaster Council Home Improvement Agency — free advice on adapting your home, plus help with the grant application paperwork.
  • VAT relief — if the person using the bathroom is disabled or has a chronic condition, the labour and some materials on a mobility-bathroom conversion can be zero-rated for VAT (a 20% saving). HMRC form VAT1615A. We handle the paperwork for you.

Doncaster-Specific Things to Watch For

A few local factors that shape every job we do in this area:

Older terraces with microbore pipework. Common across Hexthorpe, Bentley and Mexborough. Microbore can struggle to deliver decent shower flow. Part of our survey is checking flow rate at the bathroom — if it's too low for a comfortable shower, we'll tell you up front and quote for either a pipework upgrade or a pumped solution (£200-£450 typical extra).

Doncaster hard water. Limescale eats shower fittings if you don't pick the right ones. We fit fittings rated for hard-water areas as standard, and recommend the right cleaning products to keep them looking new for years.

Floor strength in older properties. Some older terraces have weaker bathroom floors than modern wet-room work needs. Part of the survey is checking — if reinforcement is needed, we tell you in the quote, not on day 2.
