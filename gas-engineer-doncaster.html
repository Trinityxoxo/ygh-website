Fully Qualified Gas Safe Engineers

Gas safety is no joke. Faulty gas appliances can cause carbon monoxide poisoning, explosions, and fires. Yorkshire Green Heating employs only fully qualified, Gas Safe registered engineers who understand the critical importance of proper installation, servicing, and safety checks. We take gas safety as seriously as you should.

Whether you need a safety certificate, boiler servicing, or a new gas appliance installed, our team has the qualifications and experience to do it right. We work to the highest safety standards and carry all necessary certifications and insurance.

Gas Safety Certificates (CP12)

For Landlords

If you rent out a property, you're legally required to have all gas appliances inspected annually by a Gas Safe registered engineer. We issue CP12 Gas Safety Certificates confirming that all appliances are safe. These certificates are essential for your legal obligations and protecting your tenants.

For Homeowners

Even if you're not a landlord, regular gas safety checks are highly recommended. We can inspect your boiler, gas fire, gas cooker, and other appliances, issuing a certificate confirming everything is safe and operating correctly.

Gas Fire Servicing

Gas fires provide warmth and ambiance, but they require proper servicing. We clean, inspect, and test your gas fire to ensure it operates safely and efficiently. Annual servicing is recommended to prevent problems and maintain efficiency.

Gas Cooker Installation

Installing a gas cooker requires careful attention to safety. We handle connection to your gas supply, ensuring proper venting and all safety features work correctly. Only a qualified gas engineer should install gas cookers—never attempt DIY.

Annual Boiler Servicing

Your boiler is a critical piece of equipment. Annual servicing keeps it safe and efficient. We provide a combustion-led Boiler Service from £90 inc VAT, scheduled for 40 minutes on site — visual inspection, operational and pressure checks, calibrated flue gas analysis (BS 7967), and a written service report. A full strip-down and component clean is only carried out if combustion readings fall outside manufacturer tolerance, in line with Gas Safe TB 143. Full scope and pricing on our Terms & Conditions page.

Carbon Monoxide Safety

Carbon monoxide (CO) is a silent killer—odorless, colorless, and deadly. All gas appliances produce some CO, but proper installation and servicing ensures it's safely vented away. We test for CO leaks and advise on carbon monoxide detectors. If we detect any issues, we address them immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I have my boiler serviced?
Annual servicing is highly recommended. Ideally, service your boiler before winter so you're covered for the heating season. Regular servicing prevents breakdowns and maintains efficiency.
What's the difference between a boiler service and a CP12 certificate?
Our Boiler Service is a combustion-led check — visual inspection, operational and pressure checks, and calibrated flue gas analysis to verify the boiler is burning safely and within manufacturer tolerance. A full strip-down is only carried out when combustion readings indicate it's needed. A CP12 (Landlord Gas Safety Certificate) is a separate, statutory safety inspection of all gas appliances in a rental property — landlords are legally required to renew it annually. They're related but different documents and you may need both.
How can I tell if my boiler is safe?
Annual servicing and CP12 checks are the best way. Warning signs include strange noises, yellow flames (should be blue), or a burning smell. Call us immediately if you notice these.
Is a CP12 certificate legally required?
Yes, if you rent out a property, you must have a CP12 issued annually by a Gas Safe engineer. For homeowners, it's not required by law but highly recommended for safety.
What should I do if I smell gas?
Leave the building immediately and call us or the National Gas Emergency Service. Don't use electrical switches, create sparks, or re-enter the property until it's been checked.
