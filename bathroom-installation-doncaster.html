Transform Your Bathroom

Your bathroom is a personal sanctuary. It should be beautiful, functional, and perfectly tailored to your lifestyle. Whether you're planning a complete bathroom renovation or just updating fixtures, Yorkshire Green Heating brings expertise, attention to detail, and genuine care to every project. We handle everything from initial planning and design advice through to final installation and finishing touches.

We've completed hundreds of bathroom projects across Doncaster. From compact en-suites to spacious family bathrooms, we have experience with every style and configuration. Our team works efficiently to minimize disruption, maintains high standards of cleanliness throughout the project, and leaves your bathroom looking stunning.

What We Offer

Full Bathroom Renovations

Starting from scratch? We handle the complete transformation. This includes removing old fixtures, updating plumbing and electrics, installing new suites, tiling, and all finishing work. We'll advise on layout, materials, and design to create a bathroom you'll love.

En-Suite Installation

En-suites add value and convenience to any home. We can create a beautiful en-suite from an existing room, carving out space where you thought there wasn't any. Complete plumbing and drainage work included.

Shower Room Installation

Streamlined and stylish. Shower rooms are perfect for busy households, compact spaces, or as a second bathroom. We install everything from walk-in showers to compact shower cubicles.

Wet Rooms

Modern, accessible, and luxurious. Wet rooms feature seamless flooring and drainage. They're ideal for contemporary homes and provide excellent accessibility. We handle all the technical aspects including waterproofing and drainage.

Our Bathroom Installation Process

Step 1: Design Consultation — We discuss your vision, budget, and timeline. We'll suggest layouts and materials based on your space and preferences.

Step 2: Quotation — You'll receive a detailed quote breaking down all costs. We're transparent about what's included and any potential variations.

Step 3: Project Planning — We schedule the work, arrange delivery of materials, and plan minimizing disruption to your home.

Step 4: Installation Phase — Our team manages demolition, plumbing, electrical work, tiling, and fixture installation. We maintain a clean, professional worksite throughout.

Step 5: Finishing & Final Checks — All fixtures are tested, sealants are applied, and final touches are completed. We ensure everything works perfectly.

Step 6: Handover — We show you how to use all features, provide care instructions, and ensure you're completely satisfied.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a bathroom installation take?
Most bathroom renovations take 1-3 weeks depending on size and complexity. A simple suite change might take a few days. We'll give you a clear timeline.
What's included in your bathroom service?
Demolition, plumbing, electrics, suite/fixture installation, tiling, sealing, and all finishing work. We handle everything except decoration (unless requested).
Can you work with my existing budget?
Absolutely. We work at all budget levels. We'll recommend solutions that give you the best value for your investment.
Do you handle wet rooms and waterproofing?
Yes. We're experienced with wet room installation including all waterproofing requirements for perfect drainage and safety.
Can you relocate pipes for a better layout?
Yes. We can reconfigure plumbing to achieve your desired bathroom layout. We'll advise if any limitations exist.
