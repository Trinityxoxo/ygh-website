5.Day-to-Day Protocol

  • Every morning: "Good morning" in the team WhatsApp — confirms you're ready, keeps the day visible. Check ServiceM8 jobs and your calendar.
  • Availability: calendar synced with ServiceM8 at all times; block unavailable time in advance.
  • On site: mark Start Job / On Site in ServiceM8 · before/after photos of every worked-on area · clear notes (fault, work done, follow-ups) · update job status before leaving · tag Adam or the office to escalate.
  • With customers: professional greeting · offer to remove shoes · plain English, no jargon · walk them through the work · ask for the review — it activates their 12-month guarantee.
  • Communication: issues get flagged early, not explained late. Nobody gets a hard time for raising a problem.

6.Invoicing & Payment

  • Invoices reference job numbers, submitted by Friday 5:00 PM.
  • Standard payment: 7 days from a valid invoice.
  • Complete documentation = on-time payment. The loop in section 3 is what gets you paid.
