Take Control of Your Heating

A smart thermostat is one of the simplest upgrades you can make to a modern heating system — and one of the few that genuinely pays you back. Instead of guessing with a dial on the wall, you control your heating from your phone, set schedules around your real routine, and only heat your home when you actually need it. Yorkshire Green Heating supplies and fits the leading smart thermostats across Doncaster, properly wired and set up so they work the way they should from day one.

Because we're Gas Safe registered heating engineers, we don't just bolt a gadget to the wall — we make sure it's correctly integrated with your boiler and heating controls, then show you how to get the most out of it.

Smart Thermostats We Install

Google Nest

Learns your routine and adjusts automatically, with clear energy history so you can see where you're saving. A popular, intuitive choice for most homes.

Hive

Simple, reliable app control with scheduling, geolocation, and easy add-ons like smart radiator valves. A great all-rounder.

Tado & Others

Room-by-room control, open-window detection, and weather adaptation. We'll talk you through the options and recommend the best fit for your home and budget.

How a Smart Thermostat Saves You Money

Heat only when you need it: Schedules and geolocation mean the heating isn't running in an empty house.

Room-by-room control: Paired with smart radiator valves, you can heat the rooms you're using and turn the rest down.

See your usage: Energy reports show you exactly what's costing you, so you can make small changes that add up.

Never come home to a cold house: Turn the heating on from your phone on the way home — or off when you've forgotten.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will a smart thermostat work with my boiler?
The vast majority of modern boilers and heating systems work with smart thermostats. We'll check your current setup before we quote and recommend the right model so it integrates cleanly.
How much can I really save?
Savings vary by home and habits, but heating only when you need it — rather than running on a timer regardless — typically trims a meaningful amount off your heating bills, especially when paired with smart radiator valves.
Can you supply the thermostat or should I buy my own?
Either works. We can supply and fit the right model for you, or install one you've already bought. We'll let you know if it's a good match for your system first.
Will you set up the app for me?
Yes. We configure the thermostat, connect it to your Wi-Fi, set up your schedule, and show you how to use the app before we leave.
Which areas do you cover?
We cover Doncaster and the surrounding DN postcodes, including Balby, Bessacarr, Cantley, Armthorpe, Bentley, Mexborough, Conisbrough, Thorne, Tickhill, Rossington, and Bawtry.
