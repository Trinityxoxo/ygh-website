What We Repair

We work on every major boiler brand including Worcester Bosch, Vaillant, Ideal, Baxi, Glow-worm, Viessmann, Potterton, Vokera and more. Common faults we fix routinely across Doncaster homes:

  • Pressure loss / boiler losing pressure overnight — usually an expansion vessel, PRV (pressure relief valve), or a leak somewhere in the system
  • No hot water (heating still working) — typically a failed diverter valve
  • No heating and no hot water — could be a stuck pump, sticky diverter, PCB fault, or low pressure
  • F-codes / error codes on the display — each brand has its own fault codes; we know what they mean and what they actually cost to fix (not always what the code suggests)
  • "Kettling" / banging / knocking noises — often limescale build-up on the heat exchanger (very common in Doncaster's hard-water area), sometimes a faulty pump
  • Pilot light keeps going out — typically a thermocouple or flame sensor
  • Boiler keeps cutting out / short-cycling — could be a temperature sensor, gas valve, or system flow issue
  • Leaking boiler — diagnose the source first, never just patch over
  • Fan / flue fault — fans are common after 7–10 years of use
  • Frozen condensate pipe — winter classic, often a free fix on a call-out

Repair or Replace? The Rule of Thirds

If a single repair would cost more than one third of a new boiler, replacement is usually the better long-term call. A typical mid-range new combi in Doncaster is £2,400–£3,000 fully fitted, so any single repair above ~£800 is worth running through the rule of thirds.
