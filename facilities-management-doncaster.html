Our Commercial Services

Planned Preventative Maintenance (PPM)

Scheduled servicing of boilers, commercial heating systems and associated plant, set to a calendar that suits your sites. Regular maintenance reduces breakdowns, extends equipment life and keeps you ahead of compliance deadlines.

Commercial Boiler Installation & Repair

Supply, installation, upgrade and repair of commercial and light-commercial boilers and heating systems. We advise on the most efficient option for the building and manage the work around your operating hours.

Gas Safety Certificates — CP12 & LGSR

Statutory gas safety inspections and certification across single buildings or whole portfolios. We track renewal dates for you so a certificate never expires unnoticed.

Reactive Repairs & Call-Outs

Fast local response when heating or hot water fails. Being Doncaster-based means we can get to your site quickly and keep downtime to a minimum.

Multi-Site & Portfolio Contracts

One agreement, one point of contact and consistent paperwork across every building you manage — with reporting that makes your own compliance records simple to maintain.

Plant Room & System Upgrades

Assessment and improvement of ageing plant, controls and distribution — helping older commercial buildings run more efficiently and more reliably.

Step 1: Site Assessment — We visit your building or buildings, review the existing plant and your compliance position, and understand how you work.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work with facilities management companies as a subcontractor?
Yes. We regularly act as the heating, gas and plumbing contractor for facilities managers across Doncaster and South Yorkshire. You get a Gas Safe registered, fully insured local team that is easy to schedule against your planned maintenance calendar.
Can you cover a portfolio of buildings on one contract?
Yes. We set up multi-site and portfolio contracts with a single point of contact, consistent paperwork and reporting across every building, and tracked renewal dates for gas safety certificates.
Do you issue Landlord Gas Safety Records (CP12)?
Yes. We carry out statutory gas safety inspections and issue CP12 / LGSR certificates for landlords and letting agents, and we can send renewal reminders so certificates never lapse.
Are you insured for commercial work?
We carry £5 million public liability cover and are fully Gas Safe registered (638592). Documentation is available for your compliance records.
How quickly can you respond to a breakdown?
We are Doncaster-based, so for reactive call-outs across Doncaster and the surrounding DN postcodes we can usually attend quickly. Response times and priority can be agreed as part of your contract.
Are you VAT registered and able to invoice businesses properly?
Yes. Yorkshire Green Heating Ltd is VAT registered and set up for business clients, with proper VAT invoicing. We are registered at Companies House under number 12273069.
