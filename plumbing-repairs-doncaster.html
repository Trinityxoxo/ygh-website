Common Plumbing Repairs We Handle

Leaks & Burst Pipes

Leaks waste water, damage your home, and only get worse if ignored. We trace the source, fix it at the root, and check the surrounding pipework so it doesn't come straight back. For burst pipes we aim to attend the same day wherever we can.

Dripping & Faulty Taps

A worn washer or cartridge is one of the most common calls we get. We repair or replace taps of all types — kitchen, bathroom, mixer, and traditional — and can fit a new tap you've bought yourself if you prefer.

Toilets That Won't Stop Running

Running, leaking, or slow-filling toilets waste a surprising amount of water. We fix flush mechanisms, fill valves, and seals, or replace the whole unit if it's beyond economical repair.

Sinks, Waste & Blockages

Slow or blocked sinks, basins, and waste pipes are no match for the right tools. We clear the blockage and check the run so it drains properly again.

Outside Taps, Stopcocks & Valves

We fit and repair outside taps, replace seized stopcocks, and install isolation valves so future repairs are quicker and cleaner.

Bathroom & Kitchen Plumbing

Beyond repairs, we handle the plumbing side of kitchen and bathroom work — fitting taps, sinks, dishwashers, washing machines, and waste connections. If you're planning something bigger, take a look at our bathroom installation service.
