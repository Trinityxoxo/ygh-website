Making Bathing Safe and Comfortable Again

Climbing in and out of a bath can become difficult, worrying, or simply unsafe. A walk-in shower removes that worry. There is no high bath side to step over — just an easy, level way to wash, with room to move and somewhere to sit if you need it.

Yorkshire Green Heating is a family-run Doncaster business with over ten years' experience, and we have helped many local people make their bathrooms easier and safer to use. We take our time, we explain everything in plain words, and there is never any pressure. We are here to help you stay comfortable and independent at home.

What We Do for You

We carefully remove your old bath and fit a proper, safe walk-in shower in its place. Depending on what suits you best, that can include:

  • Bath removal — we take out the old bath cleanly and tidily
  • Walk-in showers — easy, level access with no high side to climb over
  • Level-access shower trays & wet rooms — step straight in, no barrier
  • Sturdy grab rails — fitted exactly where you need them
  • Non-slip flooring — for safer footing, every day
  • Comfortable seating & easy controls — a fold-down or fixed seat, and clear, easy-to-reach taps
