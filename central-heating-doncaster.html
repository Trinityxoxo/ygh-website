Complete Central Heating Solutions

A warm home is a happy home. Whether you need new radiators, a complete heating system overhaul, or just some maintenance work, Yorkshire Green Heating has the expertise to keep your home at the perfect temperature. We design, install, and maintain central heating systems that are efficient, reliable, and tailored to your home's specific needs.

From initial planning through to installation and ongoing maintenance, we handle every aspect of your central heating. Our team will assess your home, recommend the best solutions, and carry out all work to the highest professional standards.

Radiator Installation & Replacement

Designer Radiators

Modern homes deserve modern heating. Designer radiators come in countless styles to match any décor. From sleek vertical rails to horizontal panels, designer radiators provide excellent heating while looking stunning. We supply and install radiators from leading manufacturers.

Column Radiators

Column radiators combine classic style with excellent heat output. They're perfect for period properties or anyone wanting that traditional look. Our team can install them in virtually any location in your home.

Standard Panel Radiators

Reliable, efficient, and affordable. Standard panel radiators remain the most popular choice for many homes. We can supply and fit them throughout your property.

Smart Thermostat Installation

Why waste heat? Smart thermostats let you control your heating from your phone, set schedules, and learn your preferences. We install leading brands including Nest, Hive, and Tado. Smart thermostats can reduce your heating bills by up to 15%.

Powerflush & System Cleaning

Over time, debris and sludge build up in heating systems. This reduces efficiency and can cause cold spots in radiators. A powerflush cleans the entire system, removing all deposits. The result? Better heat distribution, improved efficiency, and lower bills. We recommend a powerflush every 5-10 years.

Full Central Heating Installation

Planning a new heating system? We design complete installations tailored to your home. We'll assess your insulation, room sizes, and heating needs to create the perfect system. Installation includes new boiler, radiators, pipework, thermostat, and all controls.

Underfloor Heating

Imagine warm floors throughout your home. Underfloor heating (UFH) provides even warmth, looks invisible, and is incredibly efficient. Perfect for new builds or renovations. We design and install both wet (water-based) and dry (electric) underfloor heating systems.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a new radiator cost?
Radiator prices vary based on size, type, and brand. Standard radiators typically cost £150-£400 installed. Designer radiators can range from £300-£800+. We provide free quotes showing exact costs.
Why is one radiator cold?
Common causes are air trapped in the system (we can bleed it), sludge buildup (we can powerflush), or a faulty thermostatic valve. We'll diagnose the problem and fix it.
Will smart thermostats really save me money?
Yes. Smart thermostats can save 10-15% on heating costs by optimizing when your heating runs and learning your preferences. They also give you control from your phone.
How often should I service my heating system?
We recommend annual servicing, ideally before winter. Regular maintenance prevents breakdowns, keeps efficiency high, and extends system lifespan.
Can you install heating in my extension?
Absolutely. We can extend your existing heating system or design a new section for your extension. We'll ensure perfect balance with your existing radiators.
